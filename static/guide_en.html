📖 Online AHP Survey Auto-Generation & Deployment Guide

This service connects to your Google Spreadsheet in real-time to securely collect response data. Follow the guide below to complete your survey!

⚠️ [Core] Create Google Spreadsheet & Set Permissions (Required)

First, you need a new Google Spreadsheet to save the responses.

  1. Go to Google Drive (PC environment recommended) and create a new spreadsheet.
  2. Click the [Share] button on the top right.
  3. Paste the service account email below into the "Add people and groups" field:
    ahp2-75@ahp2-486703.iam.gserviceaccount.com
  4. Set the permission to [Editor] and click [Send].
  5. Copy the entire URL of the spreadsheet. (You must paste this URL in Section 7.)

1️⃣ Section 1 ~ 2: Survey Basic Info & Period

  • Write the survey title and purpose instructions. (This is the first screen respondents will see.)
  • Set the start and end dates. (Responses outside this period will be blocked.)

2️⃣ Section 3: Respondent Demographics (Groups)

  • Configure questions for statistical analysis, such as affiliation, age group, etc.
  • Enter options separated by commas (,). (e.g., 20s, 30s, 40s)

3️⃣ Section 4 ~ 5: AHP Model (Evaluation Criteria)

  • Enter the Main Criteria. (At least 2 required)
  • Enter the Sub Criteria belonging to each main category.
  • It is recommended to keep criteria names intuitive and concise.

4️⃣ Section 7: [Important] Spreadsheet Link & Save

  • Paste the Google Spreadsheet URL you copied earlier into the input field.
  • Click [Preview & Data Verification] to ensure there are no errors.
  • If verified, click [Link Google Sheet & Deploy Survey] to finalize.

5️⃣ Monitor Real-time Responses

  • Upon successful deployment, a shortened link will be generated. Share this link with respondents.
  • Go to the [Real-time Responses] tab at the top to monitor collected data and download analysis files.
